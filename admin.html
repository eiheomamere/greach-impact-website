How to Connect Supabase in 4 Quick Steps:

  1. Create a free project at supabase.com (Free forever, includes 500MB PostgreSQL database).
  2. In your Supabase project, click SQL Editor, open supabase_schema.sql, paste the SQL, and click Run.
  3. Go to Project Settings → API, copy your Project URL and anon key.
  4. Paste them above, click Save Credentials, and click Upload All Local Data to Supabase. All your students, prices, and blogs will immediately sync to the cloud!
