Who We Are

Greach Impact was established in 2025 by Emmanuel Iheomamere with a clear, structural conviction: access to contextual data literacy and digital tools can fundamentally transform communities and businesses.

We work across three critical implementation channels: Data Analytics & Workforce Training, Agriculture & Agro-Processing Support, and grassroots Community Capacity Building.

Headquartered in Enugu, Nigeria, our focus relies on practical training frameworks, field tracking pipelines, and active ground-truth accountability to deliver real, measurable metrics.
