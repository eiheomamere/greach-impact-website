Who We Are

Greach Impact was founded in 2025 by Emmanuel Iheomamere with a simple belief: data and technology can change lives.

We work across three main areas: Data Analytics & Training, Agriculture & Agro-Processing, and Community Development. Our goal is to create sustainable opportunities for individuals, farmers, and organizations across Nigeria.

We're based in Enugu, Nigeria, and our work reaches communities across the country. We believe in practical solutions, hands-on training, and real impact – not just talk.

Data. Agriculture. Community.

We bring together technical skills and local knowledge to solve real problems.

What We Do

We focus on three main areas that create lasting impact.

Data Analytics & Training

We train individuals and organizations in Excel, Power BI, and data analytics – turning data into decisions.

Agriculture & Agro-Processing

We support smallholder farmers with processing solutions, market access, and agricultural data insights.

Community Development

We build capacity, create opportunities, and empower communities across Nigeria.

Consulting & Advisory

We help nonprofits, businesses, and organizations make data-driven decisions and measure their impact.
