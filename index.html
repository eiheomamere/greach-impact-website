Who We Are

Greach Impact was founded in 2023 by Emmanuel Iheomamere on a simple belief: giving people practical data skills and digital tools can change communities and businesses for the better.

We focus on two areas: Data Analytics & Workforce Training and grassroots Community Development.

Based in Enugu, Nigeria, we believe in hands-on training, real field data, and honest accountability — so the results we report are results you can actually measure.

Data. Training. Community.

Combining technical skill with real, on-the-ground understanding.

We help individuals and organizations grow through practical training and advisory services.

Data Analytics & Training
