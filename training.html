Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our training programs.

📅 What if I miss a class?
All sessions are recorded. You'll get access to the recordings so you can catch up at your own pace.
📜 Do I get a certificate?
Yes! Upon successful completion, you'll receive a certificate you can add to your CV or LinkedIn profile.
👶 Are these courses for beginners?
Yes! Our Excel Beginners, Excel Dashboard for Beginners, Power BI for Beginners, and SQL for Beginners courses are designed for beginners. The Excel Advanced course is for those with some Excel experience.
💻 What platform is used?
All live classes are held on Zoom. You'll receive links and materials before each course starts.
💰 How do I pay?
Payment details are sent after registration. We accept bank transfers and mobile payments.
🎯 Which program should I choose?
If you're new to Excel → Start with Excel Beginners.
If you know Excel basics and want to build dashboards → Choose Excel Dashboard for Beginners.
If you know Excel basics → Choose Excel Advanced.
For data visualization → Choose Power BI for Beginners.
For working with databases → Choose SQL for Beginners.
For a complete journey → Choose the Excel Complete Package.
