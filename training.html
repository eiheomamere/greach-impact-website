Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our training programs.

📅 What if I miss a class?
All sessions are recorded. You'll get access to the recordings so you can catch up at your own pace.
📜 Do I get a certificate?
Yes! Upon successful completion, you'll receive a certificate you can add to your CV or LinkedIn profile.
👶 Are these courses for beginners?
Yes! Our Excel Beginners course is designed for absolute beginners. The Advanced course is for those with some Excel experience.
💻 What platform is used?
All live classes are held on Microsoft Teams. You'll receive links and materials before each course starts.
💰 How do I pay?
Payment details are sent after registration. We accept bank transfers and mobile payments.
🎯 Which program should I choose?
If you're new to Excel, start with Excel Beginners. If you already know the basics and want to go deeper, choose Excel Advanced. For a complete journey, choose the Excel Complete Package. For data visualization, choose Power BI. For working with databases, choose SQL.
