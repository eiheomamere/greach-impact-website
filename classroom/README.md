## 📤 Capstone Project Submission & Email Delivery System

Students can submit their completed capstone project directly on the platform:
1. Student clicks **`Submit Project`** on the top bar or completes Slide 64.
2. The portal auto-fills their **Full Name**, **Student ID**, **Email**, and **Cohort**.
3. The student specifies their completed workbook file (`Master_Excel_Course_Exercises.xlsx`) and types an **Executive Project Summary** detailing their findings.
4. Student confirms the **Verification Checklist** (completed M1–M6, verified bonus quotas, 100% green auto-checks).
5. Upon clicking **`Submit Project for Instructor Review`**:
   - The platform creates a unique **Submission ID** (e.g. `#PRJ-2026-X821`) and logs it into the database.
   - **Direct Email Dispatch**: Automatically dispatches an email to the Lead Instructor (`instructor@greach.org`) containing the student's name, ID, submission ID, timestamp, and project summary.
   - The student receives an on-screen formal submission confirmation slip.
6. **Instructor Review**: The Lead Instructor can open the **`Submissions Inbox`** on the top bar, view all submissions, click **Approve**, and authorize official certification.

## 🧠 Dynamic Quizzes with Formula Hand-Coding

Unlike static multiple-choice questions, the quizzes on **Slides 13, 22, 31, 40, and 48** require students to hand-code actual Excel formulas:
* **Interactive Formula Bar (`fx`)**: Students hand-code real syntax:
  - `=SUM(A1:A10)` & `=AVERAGE(B2:B20)`
  - `=IF(B5>=10000, "Bonus", "No Bonus")` & `=TRIM(A2)`
  - `=LEFT(A2, 4)`
  - `=VLOOKUP(A2, D2:F100, 3, FALSE)` & `=XLOOKUP(A2, D2:D50, E2:E50)`
  - `=SUMIFS(D:D, B:B, "East", C:C, ">50")`
* **Smart Formula Normalization**: Accommodates spaces, uppercase/lowercase, optional leading `=`, single vs double quotes, and valid formula parameters (such as `0` vs `FALSE`).
* **Instant Evaluation & Step-by-Step Explanations**: Clicking **`Submit Quiz & Evaluate Answers`** displays:
  - Green/Red highlight per question.
  - Step-by-step **Argument Breakdown**: Explaining why the formula works, how each parameter functions, and common workplace traps.
  - Overall score badge (e.g. `🎯 Score: 3 / 3 (100%)`).
  - Retake button for unlimited practice.
